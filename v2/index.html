June 2026 · 12 days · ~2,000 km

Kenton-on-Sea
to Joburg via the Kruger

A twelve-day road trip up the spine of South Africa — Eastern Cape coast, southern Drakensberg, Maloti sandstone country, Mpumalanga highlands, the Panorama Route — then a safari split (Arathusa for Giles, Lissataba for the family), three days based at Kruger Park Lodge with self-drive Kruger trips, and the long drive home to Johannesburg.

Fri 19 Jun → Tue 30 Jun 2026 2 nights Funny Stone (Viedge Clan) 3 nights Lissataba / Arathusa 3 nights Kruger Park Lodge
The Route

At a glance

Each day is a stop on a vertical journey. The connecting line is colour-graded by altitude — coastal blue at Kenton, rust at the Maluti / Dullstroom highveld peak, Lowveld green through Hoedspruit and Kruger, then climbing rust again on the run home to Joburg.
