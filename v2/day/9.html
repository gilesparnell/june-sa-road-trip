The Point of Being Here

Self-drive Kruger, base in Hazyview

Kruger Park Lodge sits ~10 minutes from Phabeni Gate, one of three southern entrances to the Kruger National Park. The lodge itself is family-comfortable (chalets, pools, restaurants) but the real reason to be here is what's next door: three full days of self-drive Kruger, plus a Hazyview activity scene (ziplines, elephants, cultural village, river cruises) when you want a non-park day.

The split below is roughly: Sun = a Kruger day-trip (it's your first time in the park as a self-drive group); Mon = local Hazyview activities (the boys will be ready for ziplines, not another 8-hour game drive); Sat pm = settle in, swim, dinner at Perry's Bridge.

Kruger conservation fees — book the day-visit permit online ahead of time: currently ~R460 / R230 (SA residents) or ~R232 / R116 (children) per day. International visitors pay a higher band. sanparks.org/parks/kruger. The day-pass also works for re-entry through the same gate if you exit for lunch.

★ Kruger camps as day trips

Four Kruger day-loops from Hazyview

Each is a full day. Leave Hazyview by 06:30 to be at the gate when it opens (06:00 winter), and aim to be back at the gate by 17:00. Picnic spots inside the park are family-friendly; the rest camps have shops, fuel, and restaurants. Pick one or two of these for your two full days at the lodge.

Phabeni Gate → Skukuza Loop

★ Best first-timer

Phabeni → Skukuza Camp via Sand River and Sabie River roads. Phabeni is your closest gate (~10 min from KPL). Skukuza is Kruger's main camp — restaurant, museum, swimming pool, fuel.

The wildlife density along the Sabie River is genuinely high. Lions, leopards, elephants, hippos. Lunch at Cattle Baron in Skukuza, then loop back via the H4-1 (Sabie River road, animal-dense).

Numbi Gate → Pretoriuskop Loop

Quietest option

Numbi Gate → Pretoriuskop Rest Camp via the western roads. Numbi is ~25 min from KPL. Pretoriuskop is the oldest camp in Kruger — big shady trees, historic feel.

Less wildlife-dense than the Sabie River side, but quieter roads and good for sable, white rhino, and giraffe. Picnic at Pretoriuskop, loop back via the H2-2.

Paul Kruger Gate → Sabie River drive

Scenic route

Paul Kruger Gate is ~45 min via Sabie/Hazyview — drop straight onto the H4-1 Sabie River road. The Sabie River corridor is Kruger's prime wildlife real estate — leopards in riverside trees, lion prides on the banks, hippo pools. The road follows the river all the way to Skukuza (~50 km). Lunch at Skukuza, return through the same gate.

Lower Sabie distance day

Long day

Phabeni → Skukuza → Lower Sabie via H4-1, return via H1-2. Lower Sabie is widely rated the best wildlife camp in Kruger — the H4-1 east of Skukuza, and the road to Lower Sabie along the river, see leopards routinely.

Longer drive (~2.5h each way inside the park), so this is an all-day commitment. Lunch at Mugg & Bean Lower Sabie overlooking the river. Possibly too long for ten-year-olds.

Self-drive Kruger tactics: Drive slowly (40 km/h max inside the park). Watch for other cars stopped — that's the universal "something is here" signal.

Picnic spots like Tshokwane, Nkuhlu, and Afsaal have toilets, drinks, occasional grills. Carry water — there's no pump-stops between camps. Sightings app: latestsightings.com shows where animals are being reported right now.

Local activities — Hazyview

A non-Kruger day in Hazyview

After a long Kruger day, the boys will be ready for adrenaline rather than another vehicle. Hazyview's adventure scene is solid — ziplines, elephants, cultural village, river cruises.

Pool / golf / chill day at the lodge

Recovery

KPL has multiple pools, a 9-hole par-3 course, kids' areas, and on-site restaurants. After two days of game drives + a zipline session, an afternoon doing nothing is genuinely earned.

Where you're sleeping

Kruger Park Lodge — three nights

Kruger Park Lodge

Family-comfortable timeshare-style resort in Hazyview. Self-catering chalets sleep 6, multiple pools, 9-hole golf course, restaurants on-site, kids' play areas. Booking via Lekker Slaap or directly with the resort. ~10 min from Phabeni Gate.

🧒 For the boys

Kruger & Hazyview highlights

  • First self-drive Kruger sighting — the moment you stop the car for an elephant herd crossing the road is the moment safari clicks.
  • Skukuza camp lunch — proper restaurant inside the Kruger, watching hippos in the Sabie River from the deck.
  • Skyway Trails Tower Jump — biggest adrenaline hit in Hazyview.
  • Elephant Whispers tour — touch an elephant's trunk; learn how big a footprint is.
  • Shangana drumming & dance — kid-engaging cultural experience.
  • Sundowner at the pool — Hazyview evenings stay warm in June. Earn it.
