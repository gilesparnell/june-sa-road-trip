The Deadline Morning

Two stops, one airport

Leave Graskop by 7am. Two stops only — Bourke's Luck Potholes (opens 7am) and the Three Rondavels viewpoint — and you'll be at Hoedspruit Airport with comfortable time to spare. Giles steps onto the Arathusa shuttle; Jackie and the boys keep the car and drive on to Lissataba (45 min). The Panorama-west viewpoints were knocked off Tuesday afternoon, so this morning is genuinely the two-stop run, not a sightseeing marathon against the clock.

Schedule

Target arrival 11:00–11:30am

06:30

Load the car & clear the room

Last fuel check (should be full from last night). Kids in the car with rusks, juice, a blanket — they can sleep through the first 30 minutes.

07:00

Leave Graskop

North on the R532. ~35 km, ~40 min to Bourke's Luck Potholes. Well-maintained tar through forested escarpment country.

07:45

Bourke's Luck Potholes (45 min on site)

Geological highlight of the whole Panorama Route. Cylindrical river-carved formations 6m deep, 3m wide. Suspension-bridge walkways over the gorge. Kids go absolutely feral here. R55/adult entry. Leave by 08:45.

09:00

Three Rondavels Viewpoint (20 min)

~15 km north. Three 800m dolerite towers above Blyde River Canyon — third-largest canyon in the world. Quick lookout stop. Leave by 09:30.

09:30

Descend via Strijdom Tunnel — ~1.5 hrs

Drop ~1,000m off the escarpment through the Strijdom Tunnel. Vegetation changes to subtropical bushveld, temperature rises 10°C, baboons appear at the roadside. Continue via R36/R531.

11:00–11:30

Hoedspruit Airport drop-off

Giles steps onto the Arathusa shuttle (departs 12:00 — confirm with the lodge the day before). Jackie + boys keep the car, refill the snack tub, drive on to Lissataba (45 min).

If running late at 09:00 (still at Bourke's Luck): skip Three Rondavels. The drive to the airport is the non-negotiable. Bourke's Luck is the more memorable stop for the boys anyway.

If running early at 09:00 (already past Three Rondavels): the airport has a decent café and the boys will be ready to settle. Don't add stops because you have time — use it.

★ After the airport drop — for Jackie + boys

Wednesday afternoon options

Lissataba check-in is typically 14:00 — a 12:00 airport departure leaves ~2 hours of margin. If the boys aren't done with the road, one of these is a great way to spend it. All are on the way to Lissataba.

Verified

🧒 For the boys

Day 6 highlights for ten-year-olds

  • Bourke's Luck Potholes — suspension bridges over the carved rock potholes. Kids go feral.
  • Three Rondavels — 800m dolerite towers above the third-largest canyon in the world.
  • Strijdom Tunnel descent — 1,000m drop off the escarpment in 30 minutes. Vegetation changes, temperature jumps, baboons appear.
  • HESC cheetahs (afternoon, after airport drop) — close-up endangered species, 2h tour.
  • Arrival at Lissataba — game-reserve gate, time to swap road-trip mode for safari mode.
