★ Panorama spots from Graskop

Four options — pick two or three

All four are within 30 minutes of Graskop. Order them however the morning's energy dictates — Graskop Gorge is the obvious afternoon anchor because it eats two hours and the boys will want time on the ziplines.

Graskop Gorge Lift Adventures

★ Headline

The big-ticket Graskop activity. Glass-fronted viewing lift drops 51m into the gorge to a boardwalk through indigenous rainforest. On top of that: the Big Swing (70m freefall over the gorge), a zipline foefie-slide across the canyon, aerial cable trail. Pick a combo — kids will lose their minds on at least one of these. 5 minutes from Graskop town.

Lisbon Falls (44m, double-drop)

Panorama classic

The most-photographed falls on the Panorama Route — twin streams plunging side-by-side into a basalt amphitheatre. ~15 min north of Graskop on the R532. Short walk from the parking to the viewpoints; safe for kids if you watch the cliff edge. 30 minutes start-to-finish.

💰
~R20 / R10 entry verify
📍
Graskop → Lisbon Falls

Berlin Falls (80m single drop)

Most dramatic

A single 80m straight drop through a narrow basalt funnel into a deep pool. ~5 km further north than Lisbon — easy combine. The viewpoint is a short walk from the car park. The geology makes this one the most "wow" of the local falls.

💰
~R20 / R10 entry verify

Graskop → Berlin Falls

Mac Mac Pools — picnic + swim

Kid favourite

~12 km south of Graskop on the R532 — natural rock pools fed by the Mac Mac River, just upstream from the falls you saw on Day 5. Picnic tables, braai spots, a shallow pool the boys can paddle in. Bring towels and a picnic from Graskop Spar. 60–90 minutes. Cold water but worth it on a sunny winter day.
