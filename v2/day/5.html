Day 5 · for the boys

Today's the SHOWCASE day — it's giving everything. Climb Long Tom Pass (some absolutely delulu soldiers dragged a cannon up this mountain to escape the British — no cap, respect the aura). Then pan for actual gold at Pilgrim's Rest, then God's Window where the cliff drops 700m straight down. Peak day.

🛣️ The Drive

The easiest day, with a payoff

Short driving, big rewards. Leave Dullstroom by 8am, climb Long Tom Pass with a proper cannon stop, drop into Sabie for an early lunch and the Mac Mac Falls roadside viewpoint, then visit Pilgrim's Rest — the perfectly preserved 1873 gold-rush village — before rolling into Graskop around 2pm.

Check in, drop bags, and head out late afternoon for the Panorama-west golden-hour loop — Pinnacle Rock, God's Window, and Wonder View. All within 25 minutes of Graskop, and at their best in the 4–5pm light. Back to the guest house by 6pm for braai. Tomorrow's a second Graskop day — chill mode, deeper Panorama coverage — before the Wednesday-morning HDS departure.

Stops, in order

Monday — morning to early afternoon

~09:00

⛰️ Long Tom Pass — Devil's Knuckles cannon

The R37 climbs over 2,150m past a life-size bronze cannon replica at Devil's Knuckles. 5-minute walk from the car park — the boys can climb on it.

⚙️ Did you know?

The Boers dragged a 38kg-shell cannon up this mountain to escape the British

In September 1900 General Louis Botha retreated here with two French-made Creusot siege guns — 155mm monsters that could hurl a 38kg shell 10km. Rather than surrender the guns, Botha's men dragged both cannons up the near-vertical face of Mauchsberg before the British could capture them. The British had nicknamed them the "Long Toms." The pass is named after this retreat.

~11:30

Sabie — early lunch

The Wild Fig Tree or Woodsman are reliable family options. Sabie itself is a former forestry town and the heart of waterfall country.

~12:15

💧 Mac Mac Falls

Roadside viewpoint, no hiking needed. 65m double waterfall — the mist rising from the gorge is impressive. Named after Scottish miners who all had "Mac" in their surnames.

~12:45

Pilgrim's Rest village

The 1873 gold-rush village frozen in time. Declared a national monument; the state bought the original buildings, so what you walk through is the actual town — Royal Hotel, Anglican church, jail, stamp mill.

Boys can pan for gold at the demonstration claim (operators salt it — kids always find something). Allow 1–1.5 hours. 17 km west of Graskop on the R533.

~14:15

Graskop arrival & check-in

17 km up the R533 from Pilgrim's Rest. Settle in, fuel the car for tomorrow, grab proper coffee or a stack at Harrie's Pancakes on the main street.

Late afternoon — Panorama-west golden-hour loop: Pinnacle Rock (20 min) → God's Window (30 min) → Wonder View (15 min). 60 km loop on the R534/R532 north and west of Graskop. Back to the guest house by 6pm.

🏡 Where you're sleeping

Graskop — last bed of the road trip

Graskop Hotel (centre of town, art-led, family rooms)

Long-running hotel on the main street with an art collection, decent restaurant, family rooms that sleep 4–6. Walkable to dinner; secure parking. Reliable rather than spectacular — but the staff will brief you on the Wednesday-morning Panorama timing, which matters.

graskophotel.co.za →

Misty Mountain / Summit Lodge (escarpment-edge alternative)

Self-catering cabins on the escarpment ~10 min south of Graskop, with views straight off the cliff into the Lowveld. The chairlift down to the lower viewpoints is a kid favourite. Slightly less convenient for Wednesday morning but visually unforgettable.

Day 5 highlights for ten-year-olds
