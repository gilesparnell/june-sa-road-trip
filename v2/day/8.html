The Drive

Two short legs, one family pickup

After the morning game drive at Lissataba, pack up and drive ~40 min south-west to Hoedspruit Eastgate Airport (HDS) to collect Giles off the Arathusa shuttle. From there it's a ~1.5h drive south on the R40 through White River to Hazyview, where Kruger Park Lodge sits a few minutes north of town on the R40, almost at Phabeni Gate. Aim to leave Lissataba by 11:30, lunch at HDS or White River with Giles, arrive Kruger Park Lodge ~14:30. Settle in, pool, sundowner.

Confirm with Arathusa the day before: the standard Arathusa shuttle delivers Giles back to HDS Eastgate Airport mid-morning Saturday (usually ~10:30–11:00). If timings shift, adjust the Lissataba departure. Arathusa logistics > everything else on Saturday.

Either the airport café (quick) or push 30 min south to White River for something better (Mugg & Bean / Casalinga). White River is on the way.

~50 km, 45 min. Through the timber-and-banana country between White River and Hazyview. Kruger Park Lodge is on the R40 just north of Hazyview town, near the Phabeni Gate turn-off.

Kruger Park Lodge arrival

Check in, pool, sundowner. Long afternoon to decompress before three days of Kruger drives and Hazyview activities.

★ Stops along the way

Optional Saturday detours

The drive is short, so there's room for one stop if anyone has energy. White River is the most practical, but Hazyview itself has interesting things if you arrive early.

White River — lunch and craft markets

On route

Halfway between HDS and Hazyview. The Casterbridge Centre has a craft market, gallery, and good lunch options (Pickle & Pie, Bagdad Café). Good "stretch and feed" stop.

Sabie Falls / Lone Creek Falls

Quick detour

If you've never seen them, both waterfalls are ~30 min west of Hazyview off the R536. 30-min stop apiece, easy walks. Best in winter when there's still flow.

HDS Airport café

Pragmatic

Quick lunch option if Giles's shuttle lands later than expected. The Eastgate café is decent — sandwiches, coffee, kids' menu. Reunite, eat, hit the road within an hour.

Where you're sleeping

Kruger Park Lodge — three nights

Kruger Park Lodge (Sat 27 → Tue 30)

Family-focused timeshare-style lodge in Hazyview, ~10 min from Phabeni Gate. Self-catering chalets sleeping 6, multiple pools, kids' areas, restaurants on-site. Functional rather than boutique — the appeal is location: it's the natural base for Kruger day-trips. See Day 9 for the full Kruger plan.
