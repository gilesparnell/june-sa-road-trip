The final drive — 5–6 hours back to Joburg via Long Tom Pass (you crossed it eastbound on Day 5; this time westbound) and the N4 highway. Maybe stop at the Voortrekker Monument in Pretoria if you have energy. 12 days, 5 provinces, ~2,000km — done.

The Drive

Lowveld → Highveld, 12 days finally home

The return route loops back up through familiar territory — Sabie, Long Tom Pass (you cross it again, this time east-to-west), Lydenburg, Belfast — then the N4 toll road carries you west through Middelburg, Bronkhorstspruit, Pretoria, and into Johannesburg. The biggest single change is altitude: KPL is at ~600m, JHB is at ~1,750m. You climb 1,300m over the day. Most of the climb happens between Sabie and Belfast.

Aim to leave KPL by 08:00 to be at JHB by mid-afternoon. The N4 from Belfast is a toll road and moves fast (~120 km/h limit), but you've got two ten-year-olds and a long trip behind you — plan one decent lunch stop, one short Long Tom Pass leg-stretch, and arrive Joburg ~14:30–15:00 with energy intact.

N4 tolls: The Belfast → Pretoria stretch has three toll plazas (Machado, Middelburg, Diamond Hill). Total ~R150–200 in tolls. Card or cash; cash is faster.

Schedule

Suggested Tuesday timing

07:30

Pack & check out from Kruger Park Lodge

Last poolside coffee, load the car, settle the bar tab. Top up fuel at the Engen on the R40 before leaving Hazyview.

~08:30

Hazyview → Sabie (R536)

~50 km, 45 min. Through the timber forests west of Hazyview. Sabie itself is the last "Lowveld feel" town before the climb.

~09:30

Sabie → Long Tom Pass (R37)

~50 km, 1h with stop. You'll cross the pass eastbound at the same Devil's Knuckles cannon viewpoint you stopped at last Tuesday — this time you'll appreciate the climb rather than the descent. Quick 10-min stop if anyone wants the photo on the way back.

~11:00

Lydenburg → Belfast (R37 → N4)

~100 km, 1.25h. Descend into the highveld, join the N4 westbound at Belfast. First tolls start here — Machado plaza is the first.

~12:30

Belfast / Middelburg — lunch stop

Both have decent N4 stop-overs. Middelburg has the larger Spar / Wimpy / KFC strip and a Total Stop with toilets. ~45 min stop. Fuel up — the next fuel is in Pretoria.

~14:00

Middelburg → Pretoria → Johannesburg (N4 → N1)

~220 km, 2.5h. Fast highway through the highveld. Watch for traffic into Pretoria (especially eastern suburbs); the N1 south to JHB can slow significantly after 15:00. Arrive Johannesburg ~16:00 latest.

Pretoria detour option: If you've made good time and have an hour to spare, the Voortrekker Monument is a 5-min detour off the N1 at Pretoria. Hilltop monument with a museum, panoramic views, and serious South African history — pivot point for explaining the Great Trek to the boys. Maps detour →. Skip if traffic is heavy.

★ Optional stops on the way home

Three "if you have an hour" options

All three are short — none should add more than 90 minutes to the day. The Long Tom revisit is free and on-route. The Voortrekker Monument is the only one worth a meaningful detour.

Voortrekker Monument (Pretoria)

History stop

Massive hilltop monument commemorating the Great Trek (1830s). Museum, panoramic views, cenotaph that lights up at noon on 16 December. Genuinely interesting whether you agree with the politics or not. 5-min detour off the N1.

Middelburg Total Stop — lunch break

Pragmatic

Not really an activity — but Middelburg's N4 Total Stop is the cleanest, fastest lunch + toilets + fuel stop on the highveld stretch. Kids' menu, decent coffee, secure parking. 45 min in and out.

Belfast — Sandstone Heritage Trust

Niche

Restored vintage trains, sandstone-era farm machinery, working blacksmith. Niche but excellent if any of you are railway-curious. ~30 min detour off the N4 at Belfast.

🧒 For the boys

Day 14 highlights for ten-year-olds

  • Crossing Long Tom Pass eastbound — you crossed it westbound on Day 5. Notice how different it looks from the other direction.
  • Altitude jump — Hazyview is 600m, JHB is 1,750m. Ears pop somewhere near Belfast.
  • N4 toll plazas — three of them. Kids can call out the tolls as you pass.
  • Voortrekker Monument (if you stop) — climb to the dome top for the Pretoria-and-beyond view.
  • Arrival in Joburg — 12 days, 5 provinces, ~2,000 km. Quietly remarkable, when you think about it.
