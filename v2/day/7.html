The Point of Being Here

Chill, to the max

After 7 days on the road and an airport handover, this is the genuine decompression block. The default plan is do nothing — pool, bushveld walks from the lodge, sundowners while the kids spot impala from the verandah. Lissataba's own game drives and bush activities are the headline experience; the list below is for the days you want a half-day excursion off the reserve.

Hoedspruit is one of the densest concentrations of wildlife and conservation operations in South Africa — there's something genuinely world-class within 30 min of the gate in almost every direction. Pick one or two; don't try to do all six.

★ Activities near Lissataba

Half-day excursions in the Hoedspruit area

All within 45 minutes of the lodge gate. The conservation centres are the standouts for kids — they're hands-on and educational without feeling like a school trip.

Where you're sleeping

Lissataba — three nights

Lissataba Private Reserve

Private game reserve in the Greater Hoedspruit area. Full board, twice-daily game drives, bush walks. The lodge runs its own programme — game drives at sunrise & sunset are the structural anchor of each day, and you can step off the schedule any time. Pool, decent kitchen if you do need to self-cater anything between drives.

🧒 For the boys

Days 8–10 highlights for ten-year-olds

  • The first game drive — sunset, vehicle, ranger, animals appearing out of the bush. Few things compete.
  • Bush walks at the lodge — the ranger reads tracks, dung, broken twigs. Kids find this surprisingly enthralling.
  • HESC cheetahs — meet rescued endangered species up close. 2h tour with a guide.
  • Moholoholo lion encounter — hands-on experience with rehabilitated big cats. More memorable than a zoo.
  • Khamai snake handling — hold a python under handler supervision. Bragging-rights material.
  • Pool time — winter days in the Lowveld are 25°C+ even in June. Pool is open.
